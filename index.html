ABOUT US

Landscape is more than the spaces between buildings. It is the first impression, the journey through a site, the balance between architecture and nature, and the element that gives a place its identity. A thoughtfully designed landscape enriches everyday experiences, improves environmental performance, and creates spaces that continue to evolve with time.

Founded in 2024, Design Harvest Consultants is a Kolkata-based design practice led by Architect Tiasha Banerjee, specializing in landscape architecture and façade design across Eastern India. With a team of architects and designers, we create contemporary environments that respond to their context while embracing sustainability as a fundamental design principle.

Our philosophy is rooted in the seamless interplay of land, architecture, and people. Through carefully crafted levels, fluid spatial planning, native planting, and timeless materiality, we design landscapes that are both visually compelling and ecologically responsible. Every project is an opportunity to shape experiences, strengthen the relationship between the built and natural environment, and create places that are functional, enduring, and memorable.

From residential communities and commercial developments to hospitality destinations and institutional campuses, our work spans the complete design process-from master planning and concept development to detailed construction documentation and execution support.

At Design Harvest Consultants, we believe that great landscapes are not an addition to architecture-they are an essential part of it.
